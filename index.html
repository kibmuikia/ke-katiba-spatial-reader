Constitution of Kenya 3D

An interactive, high-performance 3D spatial reader for Kenya's 2010 Constitution.

Project & Data Sources

This application structures legal provisions into relational SQLite and JSON formats, rendering vector canvas pages directly onto dynamic 3D meshes in Three.js.
